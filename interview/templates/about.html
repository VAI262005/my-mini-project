About AI Interview Performance Analyzer

The AI Interview Performance Analyzer is an intelligent placement preparation platform developed to help students improve interview skills through Artificial Intelligence. The system analyzes speech, communication, confidence, grammar, and technical responses, providing HR-style feedback to prepare candidates for real-world interviews.

Core Assessment Capabilities
Speech & Language NLP Extracts keywords, tracks grammatical syntax errors, and counts speech fillers like "um", "like", or "actually".
Visual Framing Analysis Checks camera eye contact focus boundaries and body posture alignment to evaluate confidence presentation.
